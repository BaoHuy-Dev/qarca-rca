# FISAT 2026 / Springer checklist

Checked against the live official pages on 2026-07-22.

- [ ] Submit to the **Final Track** by 10 August 2026.
- [ ] Confirm timezone with the conference manager; do not rely on the old
      Main Track dates.
- [ ] Use English and the Springer LNICST/Computer Science proceedings kit.
- [ ] Keep a regular paper at 12--15 body pages or a short paper at 6--11;
      references, appendices, and acknowledgements are excluded by the CFP.
- [ ] Initial PDF is anonymized; author metadata remains in Confy+.
- [ ] Confirm the anonymity conflict because the FPT mirror still says
      single-blind.
- [ ] Do not alter `llncs` margins, fonts, or spacing; do not add page numbers.
- [ ] Abstract is 150--250 words; citations use numbered square brackets.
- [ ] Every citation resolves to a verified source and DOI where available.
- [ ] Figure captions are below; table captions are above; every display is
      referenced in text and readable in grayscale.
- [ ] Supply concise plain-text alternative descriptions for every figure and
      image-based table.
- [ ] Include one corresponding author and email in camera-ready metadata.
- [ ] Include funding/acknowledgements if applicable.
- [ ] Include the mandatory Disclosure of Interests statement.
- [ ] Transparently disclose substantive generative-AI assistance; an AI tool
      cannot be an author and humans remain accountable for all content.
- [ ] Camera-ready ZIP contains the exact PDF, TeX sources, `.bib`/`.bbl`,
      class/style files, and every figure.
- [ ] At least one author completes full registration and presents the paper.
